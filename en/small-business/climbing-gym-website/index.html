What a climbing gym website should have

None of this is exotic. It's what someone expects before booking a climbing session, and almost nobody has it.

  1. 01

    Booking that actually works, not a button that leads nowhere

    The customer picks a slot and pays or reserves it right there, on your site. No commission handed to an outside platform for every pass. Keep a listing elsewhere if it brings you people, but stop being the gym where the booking button is broken.

  2. 02

    Capacity control per time slot

    Each session has a limit, and the site stops offering spots once it's full. No overbooking the wall, no two groups fighting over the same routes at the same time.

  3. 03

    Instant confirmation for the session booked

    The customer gets confirmation the moment they book. It's what cuts down the empty slots nobody flags until the day itself.

  4. 04

    Real prices and hours on the site, not hidden behind a call

    Day passes, memberships and gear hire, updated and readable on a phone. Not a PDF from two seasons ago, not a single page with no price on it at all.

  5. 05

    Actual text about what people ask before they come

    Whether a belay test is required, if gear hire is included, whether there are courses or just open access, how to get there and where to park. Written as text, not locked inside an image nobody can read on a phone.

What people usually ask me

Can I sell day passes and membership bookings without paying a platform commission?

Yes. The sale happens on your own site and the money is entirely yours, with no percentage handed to an outside platform for every pass or booking. Keep a listing on other platforms if they bring you new people, but you stop depending on them to sell.

Can I cap how many people book a time slot?

Yes. You set the maximum per session and the site stops offering spots once it's full. No overbooking the wall, no two groups fighting over the same routes at the same time.

Does the booking system sync with the till or door control?

It can be linked to whatever you already use at the door, so a booked session gets validated automatically instead of being ticked off a list by hand. It's tested in front of you before anyone signs off on it.

What happens to customers' data?

Only the minimum gets collected: name, contact details and the session chosen. The data goes to your site and your inbox, not to a middleman platform, and the legal side is agreed in writing before we start.
