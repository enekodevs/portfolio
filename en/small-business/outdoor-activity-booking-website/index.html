What an outdoor activity website should have

None of this is exotic. It's what someone expects before booking an activity with some risk attached, and almost nobody has it.

  1. 01

    Booking on your own site, not a platform taking a cut

    The customer picks a session, pays or leaves a deposit, right there. No handing a percentage of every booking to an outside platform. If a listing elsewhere brings you people, keep it, but stop being the centre where booking means calling.

  2. 02

    Instant confirmation and a reminder before the activity

    The customer gets confirmation the moment they book, and a reminder the day before with what to bring. It's what cuts down the empty slot nobody notices until the group doesn't show up.

  3. 03

    Ability to close a session for weather or water levels without confusing anyone

    If conditions turn, you close that session from your own panel and everyone who had a spot gets the message at once, instead of you calling round one by one.

  4. 04

    Real prices on the site, not a contact-us button

    A clear price per activity and per group, or the fixed price if you have one. Someone comparing five centres on their phone rules out first the one that won't say what it costs.

  5. 05

    Actual text about what people ask before booking

    Minimum age, fitness level needed, what to bring, whether the price includes gear. Written as real text, readable on a phone, not locked inside a photo that neither Google nor a screen reader can read.

What people usually ask me

Can I take a deposit or full payment when someone books?

Yes. The customer pays in full or leaves a deposit at the moment of booking, whichever you decide. The money lands straight in your account, with no middleman taking a cut of every session.

What if I need to cancel a session because of weather or water levels?

You close that session from your own panel and everyone who had a spot is notified at once, with the option to move to another date or get a refund depending on how you've set it up. No calling round one by one.

Can I limit how many people book each session?

Yes. You set the maximum per session and the site stops offering spots once it's full. No fitting more people in than a guide can safely take.

What happens to customers' data?

Only the minimum gets collected: name, contact details, and whatever the activity needs, such as weight or age where a limit applies. The data goes to your site and your inbox, not to a middleman platform, and the legal side is agreed in writing before we start.
